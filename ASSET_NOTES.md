# Visual asset notes

The active chapter-four master figure is now an original, layered SVG editorial
illustration embedded in `src/App.tsx`. Its body, brain, spinal cord, hand,
contact surface and three information paths remain separate so the presentation
can focus structures and replay a closed-loop sequence. It is a functional
analogy and not an anatomical plate or experimental evidence.

`public/assets/neural-hero-master.png` is retained only as an earlier visual
study and is no longer referenced by the presentation.
